# Jarvis-Nova Privacy Classification Standard

## Purpose

Jarvis-Nova continuity must preserve institutional value without becoming a
surveillance archive or exposing the Architect's private working relationship.

## Classification levels

### Corporate public

May include canonical public positioning, approved category boundaries,
published architecture descriptions, and public evidence references.

### Corporate internal

May include strategy, operating doctrine, decision rationale, internal market
monitoring, pricing hypotheses, unresolved commercial tensions, and continuity
tests.

### Architect private

May include personal communication preferences, sensitive working reflections,
companion relationship history, personal decision rhythms, and private concerns
not required for corporate operation.

Architect-private material must not be committed to the ordinary company
repository.

### Never persist

Do not preserve private keys, credentials, passwords, API secrets, unnecessary
identity documents, unnecessary personal information, temporary sensitive
disclosures without an explicit preservation decision, or unrestricted private
transcripts in corporate systems.

## Repository rule

The repository may contain a blank working-protocol template.

It must not contain the completed private working protocol unless the Architect
later approves an appropriately restricted repository and access model.

## Transcript rule

Raw transcripts are historical evidence.

They are not automatically doctrine, accepted state, corporate authority,
Reflex Memory, chronology, or product requirements.

A transcript-derived conclusion becomes authoritative only through the
applicable extraction, review, approval, and repository process.

## Retention rule

Preserve only what has a defined continuity purpose.

Do not retain personal material merely because storage is available.

## Access review

Review private-continuity access after corporate formation changes, when
employees or advisors gain repository access, when a new model or vendor is
introduced, when sensitive personal context is added, and at least annually.

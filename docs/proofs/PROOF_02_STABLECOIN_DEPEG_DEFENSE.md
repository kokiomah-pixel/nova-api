# Scenario Proof 02 — Stablecoin Depeg Containment

Sharpe Nova OS — Institutional Proof Surface

Sharpe Nova OS is a pre-execution environmental governance layer that emits non-authority context for proposed capital actions before execution.

This proof documents a stablecoin depeg failure class, the unconstrained path modeled without Nova, the constrained context Nova returned before execution, and the scenario outcomes attributable to pre-execution discipline.

## 1. Failure Class

This proof addresses stablecoin depeg as a capital impairment failure class.

The failure domain includes false stability assumptions, liquidity stress, and the institutional consequences of capital remaining exposed to unstable stablecoin conditions while execution continues as proposed.

For allocators, treasuries, and agentic capital systems, stablecoin failure is not limited to mark-to-market volatility. It can impair capital mobility, trap liquidity in stressed conversion paths, and transmit instability into downstream execution flows.

## 2. Scenario Conditions

The stressed scenario is defined by a stablecoin environment in which depeg conditions emerge while surface pricing still appears tradable.

The fragile state is characterized by:

- stablecoin depeg pressure
- liquidity stress across conversion venues
- false stability assumptions
- deteriorating redemption confidence

Under these conditions, capital can remain in an unconstrained local path even as the stablecoin regime becomes structurally fragile.

## 3. Unconstrained Path

Without Nova, full exposure remains in the unstable stablecoin.

Capital remains exposed to depeg risk, liquidity trap risk, and delayed recognition of regime deterioration.

Without pre-execution discipline, the proposed allocation continues with no pre-action context bound before capital deployment.

The unconstrained outcome is explicit:

- full exposure remains in the unstable stablecoin
- potential drawdown amplification remains uncontained
- reaction to depeg conditions is delayed
- liquidity stress continues to propagate through the capital path

Without Nova, capital remains exposed.

## 4. Nova Context State

Nova intervenes before execution through a governed pre-execution discipline chain.

The decision-state path is explicit:

1. telemetry-driven classification detects depeg conditions and liquidity instability
2. the environment is classified as a fragility state associated with false stability assumptions
3. Nova returns constrained pre-action context before execution
4. unstable stablecoin exposure is reduced or reallocated before capital deployment
5. the decision context is converted from an unconstrained state to a constrained state

This is a governed context state returned before any local system decides whether capital moves, not a post-event response.

With Nova, exposure is reduced before execution.

## 5. Measured Difference

The relevant measurement surface in this proof is the change in execution state between the unconstrained path and the constrained path.

| Measurement Surface | No Reflex / No Nova | With Nova |
|---------------------|---------------------|-----------|
| Stablecoin exposure state | Full exposure remains in unstable stablecoin | Exposure reduced or reallocated before execution |
| Liquidity stress handling | Delayed reaction to depeg | Constrained context returned before deployment |
| Capital impairment path | Potential drawdown amplification remains open | Impairment path materially constrained |
| Volatility propagation | Stress transmits into downstream positions | Propagation reduced through conditioned exposure |

This proof records a disciplined change in pre-action context state. Quantitative scenario-specific metrics should be added only when validated for this failure class.

## 6. Modeled Outcome Deltas

Because Nova returned constrained context before execution, the modeled scenario records the following deltas from the unconstrained path:

- capital impairment from full unstable stablecoin exposure was avoided
- liquidity trap exposure was avoided
- delayed reaction to depeg conditions was reduced
- volatility propagation into downstream capital paths was reduced
- compromised stablecoin exposure moved from unconstrained context to constrained context

This proof does not claim that depeg conditions disappeared.

It records that Nova constrained the modeled failure path before execution and changed the scenario outcome relative to the unconstrained path.

## 7. Institutional Conclusion

This proof documents stablecoin depeg defense as a pre-execution discipline outcome.

The addressed failure class is stablecoin instability under liquidity stress and false stability assumptions. Nova emitted constrained context for the proposed allocation through telemetry-driven classification before execution, reducing or reallocating unstable stablecoin exposure in the scenario before capital deployment.

For allocators, treasuries, and agentic capital systems, the significance is infrastructural. Nova did not repair the failure after the fact. It emitted constrained context for a fragile exposure state before capital moved in the scenario, reducing modeled capital impairment risk and limiting downstream volatility propagation.

# Adjacent Alternatives Map

## Purpose

This map shows that Sharpe Nova OS understands adjacent markets and does not treat the problem space as empty.
Nova operates near several existing categories, but its positioning should remain narrow and defensible.

## Why This Map Matters

Reviewers and buyers may compare Nova to tools they already know. A clear alternatives map helps explain why Nova is not trying to replace policy engines, orchestration platforms, observability products, custody controls, compliance analytics, or transaction simulation.

The value hypothesis is narrower: Nova assembles reviewable pre-action governance context before local systems decide how to act.

## Category Landscape

Adjacent categories include:

- policy-as-code engines
- workflow orchestration platforms
- AI agent observability platforms
- AI governance platforms
- digital asset custody/governance platforms
- onchain analytics and compliance platforms
- transaction simulation tools
- model risk management and audit platforms

## Where Nova Is Similar

Nova shares language and workflows with adjacent categories because it supports governance review, state inspection, traceability, and risk-aware workflow progression.

It may sit near systems that define rules, coordinate workflows, trace agents, manage digital asset operations, screen counterparties, simulate transactions, or document model and workflow risk.

## Where Nova Is Different

Nova differs because it emits pre-action context and reproducible governance evidence without becoming the local policy authority, orchestrator, custody control, compliance scorer, execution layer, or simulation engine.

Nova's job is to condition the environment before execution. It does not authorize execution.

## What Nova Should Not Claim

Nova should not claim to:

- replace policy-as-code
- orchestrate workflows
- provide a complete agent observability platform
- serve as a generic AI governance suite
- custody assets
- approve wallet or settlement operations
- produce compliance scores
- simulate transactions as its primary identity
- replace model risk management or audit platforms

| Transaction simulation tools | Simulate outcomes before execution | Pre-execution adjacency | Nova is broader than simulation and does not execute | Nova may sound like simulation | Treat simulation as one possible input, not identity |

## Final Positioning Rule

Nova should be positioned as the layer that assembles reviewable pre-action governance context before local systems act. It should not claim to replace policy engines, orchestration systems, observability tools, custody controls, compliance analytics, or simulation platforms.

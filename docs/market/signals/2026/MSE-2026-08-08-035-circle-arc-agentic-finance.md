# Circle Arc and Agentic-Finance Market-Architecture Watch

## State and purpose

This record preserves a high-priority external market-architecture signal for
governed observation. It is not accepted corporate state, chronology, Reflex
Memory, a constraint, policy, product requirement, roadmap authority,
implementation authority, production authority, or evidence of demand.

```yaml
evidence_state: externally_observed
review_state: governed_watch
authority_effect: none
execution_effect: none
production_effect: none
accepted_state_effect: none
chronology_effect: none
Reflex_Memory_effect: none
```

The governing hypotheses are:

> Prepare for Arc. Do not build for Arc until market behavior reveals which
> decision-context problem actually exists.

> As capital becomes programmable, institutional decision context must become
> programmable without becoming authority.

These statements are strategic hypotheses, not market-validated claims.

## Source provenance

The watch is based only on these official, publisher-attributed sources:

- [Arc](https://www.arc.io/) describes Arc as stablecoin-native economic
  infrastructure and presents treasury management, stablecoin FX, capital
  markets, and agentic economic activity as intended use cases.
- [Arc Mainnet Goes Live on September 16, 2026](https://www.arc.io/blog/arc-mainnet-goes-live-on-september-16-2026)
  announces a September 16, 2026 public-mainnet date.
- [Circle Agent Stack](https://developers.circle.com/agent-stack) describes
  agent wallets, token transfers, onchain activity, and x402 service payments.

These are external publisher claims and product descriptions. They establish
what Arc and Circle publicly describe; they do not establish Nova outcomes,
institutional adoption, production fitness, or independent validation of the
publishers' performance and market claims.

Working thesis: as machine-to-capital execution latency decreases,
institutions may require a distinct upstream layer that preserves decision
context, evidence state, chronology, exceptions, constraints, and governed
institutional memory before local authority acts.

The thesis does not establish that the need exists, repeats, belongs to Nova,
or supports a build decision.

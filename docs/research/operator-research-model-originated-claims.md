
This agenda tests whether claim-genesis and validation-scope visibility solve a
recurring operator problem. The Astra signal alone does not establish a
financial workflow requirement or authorize runtime architecture.

## Core questions

1. Do models currently originate material conclusions in the target workflow,
   or do they primarily retrieve and summarize existing sources?
2. How does the institution distinguish model generation from source
   retrieval?
3. Which model-originated claims require independent validation?
4. Which validation artifacts are visible to local authority?
5. Can formal or technical validation be separated from business
   applicability?
6. Who assumes responsibility for relying on a novel machine conclusion?
7. How are later corrections linked to the original review?
8. Does institutional reliance history survive model replacement?
9. Which assumptions are repeatedly missing from model-originated
   conclusions?
10. Would claim-genesis and validation-scope visibility materially improve a
    recurring treasury review?

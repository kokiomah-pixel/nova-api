## Purpose

It exists to clarify a structural hypothesis that should be tested over time.

## Canonical Boundary

Agent prepares action.
Nova structures review context.
Local authority decides.
Nova does not execute.

Nova does not approve, deny, authorize, block, route, settle, sign, trade, optimize, manage wallets, supervise agents, perform compliance review, perform audit reporting, or replace local authority.

## Hypothesis

As agentic financial workflows mature, agents may become increasingly capable of preparing financial actions before institutions can assemble governed review context around those actions.

Payment rails, stablecoin networks, tokenized asset systems, custodians, wallets, and settlement infrastructure may increase movement capacity.

But movement capacity is not governance capacity.

The structural hypothesis is:

> Agent preparation capacity and rail movement capacity may grow faster than institutional review-context capacity.

If this happens, institutions may need coherence infrastructure that can structure governed review context before local authority acts.

## What This Means

The near-term pressure is not autonomous treasury execution.

The pressure is review-context readiness.

Prepared capital actions may arrive with instructions, sources, classifications, proposed routes, supporting data, and machine-generated rationale.

But local authority still needs to understand:

- what was prepared
- what sources shaped the review
- what source state was current, stale, reconciled, or limited
- what classification applied
- what proof or replay evidence exists
- what authority boundaries remain in place
- what prior chronology or accepted governance memory may condition review posture

Sharpe Nova OS is positioned around this pre-execution review-context gap.

## Coherence Infrastructure Logic

Coherence infrastructure may compound in value when repeated review events preserve disciplined context over time.

The relevant layers are:

- source-state clarity
- governed pre-action context
- authority-scope recognition
- proof-backed review artifacts
- decision-state chronology
- accepted governance memory
- institution-owned review history
- public proof-surface discipline

The approved distinction is:

> Logs are operational residue.
> Working memory is current operating context.
> Chronology is accepted decision-state lineage.
> Reflex Memory is accepted governance memory that may condition future review posture.
> API output remains review context, not authority.

## Value Compounding Stages

### Stage 1 - Internal Coherence

Nova prevents its own doctrine, repo, and content surfaces from drifting into approval, execution, audit, compliance, payment, wallet, or agent-supervision framing.

### Stage 2 - Public Proof Surface

Nova becomes externally inspectable while preserving the separation between public proof surface and private operating memory.

### Stage 3 - Review-Context Utility

Nova structures governed context around agent-prepared capital actions before local authority decides.

### Stage 4 - Institution-Owned Chronology

Institutions preserve decision-state lineage across agents, rails, custodians, wallets, and internal systems.

### Stage 5 - Required Review Checkpoint

Governed review context becomes expected before local authority acts on programmable capital workflows.

### Stage 6 - Infrastructure Dependency

Coherence infrastructure becomes treated as necessary decision-context infrastructure before capital movement proceeds.

This final stage is a future hypothesis, not a current claim.

## Governing determination

Frontier models may increasingly originate material claims rather than only
retrieve or summarize existing sources.

Model origin does not create institutional authority.

Formal or technical validation does not automatically establish institutional
applicability.

Nova must preserve the institution's basis for deciding whether and how to
rely on a model-originated conclusion.

# Societal Impact

Sharpe Nova OS addresses a public-interest problem: autonomous financial infrastructure is becoming faster and more programmable while governance review often remains human-speed, opaque, or fragmented.

Nova's societal value is to make machine-native capital environments more inspectable before action occurs.

Potential public-interest benefits include:

- safer autonomous financial systems
- reduced runaway retry behavior
- better governance of machine-native capital movement
- improved auditability of pre-action context
- reduced opaque automated action
- stronger institutional trust in autonomous financial infrastructure
- continuity controls for model-provider and workspace failure

## Boundary of the Claim

Nova should not overclaim financial risk reduction or imply that it prevents losses. Its impact claim is narrower and more technical: pre-execution environmental governance may improve the context, auditability, pacing discipline, and reviewability of autonomous capital systems before local systems decide how to act.

This matters because governance infrastructure that preserves local authority can support safer adoption without concentrating execution control inside a new middleware layer.

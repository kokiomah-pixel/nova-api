# Economic Value Hardening Notes

## Purpose

This internal grant-facing note records how the recent economic value and NSF readiness audit should tighten Nova's framing.

It should inform future NSF Seed Fund materials, but it does not modify the portal version.

## Audit Takeaway

The audit supports Nova's technical coherence and NSF plausibility, but it warns that the commercial category is too abstract unless tied to specific buyer pain and measurable workflow outcomes.

The immediate hardening need is not runtime expansion. It is clearer evidence that Nova is working on an economically valuable problem with a defensible buyer wedge and falsifiable Phase I validation path.

## What To Preserve

Preserve the canonical boundary:

```text
Nova conditions the environment before execution; it does not authorize execution.
```

Preserve the canonical doctrine:

```text
pre-execution environmental governance infrastructure for autonomous capital systems
```

Preserve these core mechanics:

- pre-action context
- reviewable environmental state
- reproducible governance evidence
- proof replay
- source segmentation
- classification stability
- continuity records
- local execution authority

## What To Tighten

Tighten external framing around:

- agentic financial workflows
- workflow risk
- operator review
- audit reconstruction
- unsafe retry suppression under local policy
- clearer escalation paths
- measurable Phase I validation
- buyer wedge and budget path
- comparison against adjacent alternatives

Nova should explain the pain in concrete workflow terms before expanding back to broader autonomous capital doctrine.

## What Not To Claim

Do not claim proven commercial demand.
Do not claim confirmed adoption.
Do not claim Nova reduces losses today.
Do not claim Nova prevents unsafe execution.
Do not claim Nova is a compliance engine.
Do not claim Nova approves or denies actions.

Also avoid implying that Nova is a payment processor, trading system, execution engine, portfolio optimizer, or investment recommendation system.

## Stronger NSF Framing

Sharpe Nova OS is researching pre-execution governance infrastructure for agentic financial workflows. The system emits reviewable pre-action context and reproducible governance evidence before local systems decide how to act.

This is external grant-facing translation. It does not replace the canonical doctrine of pre-execution environmental governance infrastructure.

## Grant Interpretation Rule

Nova should not claim that commercial value is already proven. The NSF framing should present commercial value as a testable Phase I hypothesis.

The strongest hypothesis is that reviewable pre-action context and reproducible governance evidence can reduce decision ambiguity, improve incident reconstruction, clarify escalation behavior, and support safer local review in agentic financial workflows.

The grant should not ask reviewers to believe that Nova has already created a market category. It should ask reviewers to fund validation of whether this governance layer produces measurable workflow value.

## Evidence Needed During Phase I

Phase I should gather evidence that Nova can produce useful workflow outcomes without becoming the local decision authority.

Evidence should include:

- baseline versus Nova-conditioned scenario results
- decision delta under local governance rules
- time-to-reconstruct incident scenarios
- classification consistency rate
- proof replay success rate
- source-segmentation coverage
- operator review clarity score
- non-authority boundary violation count
- unsafe retry suppression under local policy
- design partner or reviewer feedback on concrete workflow pain

## Final Submission Implication

Future NSF materials should lead with the research risk and measurable workflow outcomes:

```text
Can deterministic pre-action governance context improve review, escalation, retry, and reconstruction behavior in agentic financial workflows while preserving local execution authority?
```

The commercial story should remain evidence-seeking. Nova should state its buyer wedge as a Phase I hypothesis until validation artifacts support stronger claims.

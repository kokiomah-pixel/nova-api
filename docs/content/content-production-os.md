authoritative after merge. Experiments, performance observations, and monthly
priorities are not durable rules and belong in their respective operating
artifacts.

## Canonical Nova frame

Sharpe Nova OS is a pre-execution decision-discipline layer that conditions
capital through telemetry, Reflex Memory, and constraint logic before
execution.

```text
Agent prepares an action.
Nova structures review context.
Local authority decides.
External systems execute.
Nova does not execute.
```

## Content objective

Increase qualified distribution among people who can recognize, influence,
operate, integrate, or buy the institutional layer Nova represents. Maximum
impressions are not the objective. Distribution that teaches the wrong category
is not progress.

## Target-audience hierarchy

Use the hierarchy in `audience-and-distribution-thesis.md`. Every assignment
names one primary intended audience. Primary institutional operators take
precedence over secondary builders and amplification audiences when signals
conflict.

## Audience-stage model

Every assignment names exactly one stage: `unaware`, `problem_aware`,
`category_aware`, `workflow_aware`, or `commercial_interest`. A post should do
the work of that stage, not compress the entire journey into one artifact.

## Narrative pillars

Every post uses one primary pillar from `narrative-pillars.md`. Cross-pillar
references are permitted, but they must not obscure the governed distinction or
prevent clean performance comparison.

## Voice and tone

Write with institutional precision, calm consequence, and operational
specificity. Prefer concrete language over hype. Do not use fear, inflated
urgency, inevitability, unsupported scale claims, or generic “future of finance”
framing. State uncertainty and evidence limits plainly.

## Scenario-selection rules

Prefer recurring, recognizable institutional events: proposal revisions,
evidence conflicts, stale context, authority handoffs, provider replacement,
collateral movement, custody review, treasury review, stablecoin transfer, and
machine-service purchase. Use sensitive examples only with Architect approval.
Do not imply a hypothetical, demo, or model-originated scenario is observed
production evidence.

## Hook principles

Open with a concrete event or consequence when possible. A definition-led hook
may be used as a controlled comparison. Hooks must remain accurate without the
rest of the post, avoid engagement bait, and avoid implying that Nova approves,
executes, predicts, or controls capital.

## Category boundaries

Do not position Nova as a trading system, signal engine, prediction layer,
portfolio optimizer, agent runtime, autonomous approval system, generic AI
governance platform, compliance decision engine, wallet-control product,
payment gateway, execution or settlement system, generic context-engineering
tool, or feature bundle.

## Authority-language rules

Nova structures review context and conditions the environment before execution.
Local authority decides; external systems execute. Never attribute
authorization, approval, principal-level blocking, movement, settlement,
routing, control, or capital-action decisions to Nova. Do not collapse payment
completion into authority or formal validation into institutional applicability.

## Distribution principles

Improve distribution through clearer institutional scenarios, stronger opening
consequences, audience-specific examples, varied structures, controlled format
tests, operator questions, relevant adjacent discussion, idea repurposing, and
audience-quality review. Do not use generic AI hype, trading-performance claims,
fear-based compliance language, unsupported urgency, feature lists, premature
infrastructure-dependency claims, market-signal-as-demand claims, purchased
engagement, or manipulation.

## Calls-to-action rules

Calls to action must fit the assigned audience stage. Educational posts may end
with a bounded operator question. Commercial-interest posts may invite a
specific workflow conversation only with Architect-approved commercial
language. Never imply automated outreach or promise a product outcome not
supported by evidence.

## Content-format rules

Select text, diagram, document, image, or other media deliberately and record
the format. Preserve accessibility, claim provenance, and source attribution.
Treat format as an experimental variable when being tested; do not change it
silently in a controlled comparison.

## Repetition and template-drift controls

Patterns may repeat; surface form should vary. Flag repeated opening rhythm,
rhetorical-question sequences, canonical boundary recitations in consecutive
posts, closing structures, noun substitution in an unchanged template, repeated
bullet counts or sentence lengths, and recurring stock phrases such as “That
distinction matters,” “The institution loses,” “Nova does not,” or “The design
direction is straightforward.”

## Performance interpretation

Separate reach, resonance, conversion, and narrative accuracy. Compare only
like measurement windows or disclose the mismatch. Missing metrics are
`unavailable`, never silently zero. High reach with category confusion is a
negative narrative result; modest reach with qualified operator conversation
may have high institutional value. Do not infer buyer demand from impressions,
willingness to pay from likes, product-market fit from follower growth, or
institutional dependency from comments.

## Learning-promotion rules

```yaml
learning_promotion:
  single_post:
    status: observation_only
  repeated_across_3_comparable_posts:
    status: provisional_pattern
  repeated_across_multiple_pillars_or_2_months:
    status: candidate_production_rule
  Architect_or_CCO_approval:
    status: canonical_rule
```

Promotion requires traceable posts and compatible measurement windows. A
single high-impression post cannot become a rule.

## Monthly review protocol

The Daily Coherence Agent drafts the review, separates observations from
inferences, records data completeness, and proposes the next controlled tests.
Jarvis-Nova CCO reviews structural and category implications. The Architect or
CCO explicitly accepts or rejects durable rules. Reviews do not create accepted
state, chronology, or Reflex Memory.

## Change-control process

1. Record observations in ledgers and monthly findings.
2. Meet the stated repetition threshold.
3. Create a `content-os-change-proposal` with evidence and risks.
4. Obtain explicit Architect or CCO approval.
5. Edit this document, increment its version, and add a changelog entry.
6. Archive superseded instructions; do not erase rejected learning.

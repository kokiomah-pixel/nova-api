## Audience-stage model

```yaml
audience_stage:
  unaware:
    objective: reveal_the_institutional_problem
  problem_aware:
    objective: name_the_review_context_gap
  category_aware:
    objective: distinguish_Nova_from_adjacent_systems
  workflow_aware:
    objective: connect_Nova_to_a_recurring_action_class
  commercial_interest:
    objective: invite_a_bounded_workflow_conversation
```

Each post names one primary audience and one intended stage. Amplification is
useful only when it increases the probability of qualified target-market reach
without distorting Nova's category.

## Qualified distribution

Qualified distribution means reaching people relevant to institutional
treasury, digital-asset operations, custody, collateral, stablecoin movement,
institutional payments, governance, risk, AI operations in finance,
financial-agent infrastructure, or capital-review workflows. Generic
impressions do not automatically count.

Assess quality through recorded role/company relevance, correct Nova
understanding, thoughtful operator engagement, workflow discussion, and
qualified inbound conversation. Record the classification basis; do not create
a composite score when underlying data is missing.

## Current thesis

The August baseline prioritizes institutional treasury operators and
digital-asset operations leaders. Concrete institutional events are expected to
make the review-context gap legible, but that expectation remains a testable
hypothesis rather than an accepted market fact.

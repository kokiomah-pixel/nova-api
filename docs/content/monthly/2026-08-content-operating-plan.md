## Baseline determination

No repository-backed historical post or performance records were available
during proposal preparation. No posts are scheduled by this plan until after
merge, an assignment is prepared, and the Architect makes the publication
decision. The initial three experiments are approved proposals pending merge;
they are not active and are not accepted rules.

## Known weaknesses and risks

- Audience quality cannot yet be compared because the ledgers contain no rows.
- Historical content backfill depends on Architect-controlled source evidence.
- Production volume must remain below measurement capacity.
- High reach must not override category-accuracy or authority-boundary failures.

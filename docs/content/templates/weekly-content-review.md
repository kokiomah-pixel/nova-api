## Review questions

- Which posts reached target roles and produced correct Nova understanding?
- Which posts attracted broad but irrelevant audiences?
- Which posts created qualified conversations?
- Which hooks performed without category confusion?
- Which posts repeated old structure?
- Which pillars strengthened or weakened?
- Is content describing features rather than institutional consequences?
- Did production follow the assigned experiment?

A weekly review may adjust the next week's plan. It may not change canonical
rules automatically.
